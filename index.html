Performance summary

Three Higher Education journeys delivered 60 of 61 emails (98.36% delivery rate) with a 63.33% open rate — well above the 20–25% B2B benchmark. Email engagement sits at 62.30%. Click-through remains low at 1.67% (click-to-open 2.63%), with the single recorded click on a preferences link.

What this means

Deliverability and open engagement are strong. The priority is converting opens into action — stronger CTAs and clearer landing relevance — while watching the one soft bounce (1.64%) and one unsubscribe (1.67%).
