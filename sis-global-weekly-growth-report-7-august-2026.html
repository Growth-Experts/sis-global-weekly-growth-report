Outbound domain configuration

To protect sisglobal.com, cold outreach sends from dedicated lookalike domains (sisglobal.co and sis-global.co) — separating outbound from day-to-day business email.

Domains are configured with SPF, DKIM and DMARC plus dedicated mailboxes to maximise deliverability. No programme can guarantee 100% inbox placement, but this significantly reduces risk to the primary company domain.

Mimecast testing note

During testing, SIS’s Mimecast environment quarantined mail from these domains because they closely resemble the primary domain. That is expected impersonation-protection behaviour, specific to SIS’s internal email security.

Once the domains are approved or whitelisted, internal testing should clear. External recipients will still evaluate mail against their own security policies.
