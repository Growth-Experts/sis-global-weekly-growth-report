Performance summary

Five campaigns sent 477 emails to 387 active leads — 328 opened (67.44%). Volume is up significantly on Week 9 (305 sent). The 67.44% open rate remains well above cold-email benchmarks, though slightly lower than last week's 74.18%, reflecting more follow-up sends in the mix. BC UK produced the week's only reply. Bounces rose to 9 (2.33%), driven primarily by EDUCAUSE Cold.

What this means

Engagement is strong at scale. The priority now is converting the open rate into replies — refining subject lines, call-to-action copy, and follow-up timing. The EDUCAUSE bounce rate (4.97%) warrants a list-hygiene pass before the next send cycle.
