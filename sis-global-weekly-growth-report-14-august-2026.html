What we need to set up

Smartlead is live for EDUCAUSE Cold and HE US Recruitment. The remaining step in the outbound stack is Power Automate — so opens, replies, bounces and status changes flow back into Dynamics 365 as the system of record.

Without this write-back, campaign activity stays in Smartlead only. Connecting it keeps CRM timelines accurate and makes reporting consistent week to week.

Priority for next week

Configure Power Automate to sync Smartlead campaign outcomes into Dynamics 365 — contact activity, reply status and key engagement signals.

Once live, every outbound campaign (including HE US Recruitment stats from next week) will update CRM automatically.
