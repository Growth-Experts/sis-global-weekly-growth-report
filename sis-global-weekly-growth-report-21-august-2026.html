What we need to set up

Smartlead is live for EDUCAUSE Cold, HE US Recruitment and HE UK Recruitment. The remaining step in the outbound stack is Power Automate — so opens, replies, bounces and status changes flow back into Dynamics 365 as the system of record.

Without this write-back, campaign activity stays in Smartlead only. Connecting it keeps CRM timelines accurate and makes reporting consistent week to week.

This week’s progress

Met with Jac du Toit this week to discuss setting up Power Automate to sync Smartlead campaign outcomes with D365.

We’ve shared the initial flow and data requirements with SIS and are awaiting their feedback before briefing Jac to build the integration.
