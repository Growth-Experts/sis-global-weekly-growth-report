What we need to set up

Smartlead is live for EDUCAUSE Cold, HE US, HE UK, and Business Central US and UK. The remaining step in the outbound stack is Power Automate — so opens, replies, bounces and status changes flow back into Dynamics 365 as the system of record.

Without this write-back, campaign activity stays in Smartlead only. Connecting it keeps CRM timelines accurate and makes reporting consistent week to week.

This week’s progress

Business Central US and UK outreach campaigns launched this week. Five Smartlead campaigns are now live.

Next week we finalise Power Automate so campaign outcomes write back to Dynamics 365 across all live sequences.
